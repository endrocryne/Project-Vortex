Connect VortexLink Ground Station

On the next screen you will see a list of all devices connected to your computer. Ensure your VortexLink Ground Station has a clear view of your rocket and is properly connected to your computer using a data cable. Select VortexLink Ground Station ####, where #### is the id printed on the housing of your ground station. Once connected, your ground station will boot up and attempt to establish a connection with your Vortex rocket. Please note only Vortex 1 and up rockets are supported, meaning Vortex Lite is not compatible with VortexLink.
